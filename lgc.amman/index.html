PRÜFUNG
ÖSD · Österreichisches Sprachdiplom

Certified in Amman.
Recognized worldwide.

The ÖSD is Austria's official German language diploma — accepted by universities, employers, and embassies across the German-speaking world. LGC is an authorized ÖSD examination center, which means you prepare and sit your exam under one roof.

  • All levels, one place

    ÖSD A1, A2, B1, B2 and C1 exams scheduled year-round at LGC.

  • Prep that mirrors the exam

    Modules for Schreiben & Sprechen and Lesen & Hören — timed tasks, real format, honest feedback.

  • Embassy translations included in the journey

    When your certificate needs sworn translation for the German Embassy, we handle it in-house.
